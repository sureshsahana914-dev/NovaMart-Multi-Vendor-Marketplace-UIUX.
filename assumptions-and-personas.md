# Research, Assumptions, and Personas

**Note:** These are initial design hypotheses, not validated research findings. Update them after actual interviews, surveys, or usability sessions.

## Customer
**Goal:** find products quickly and feel confident about seller quality, delivery, and payment.
**Potential friction:** too many filters, unclear delivery costs, surprise fees, uncertain order status.
**Design opportunities:** clear search, useful filters, transparent pricing, seller information, predictable checkout, tracking timeline.

## Small business vendor
**Goal:** register a store, list products, fulfill orders, and understand sales performance.
**Potential friction:** confusing KYC, unclear approval status, repetitive product entry, low-stock surprises.
**Design opportunities:** step-by-step onboarding, upload feedback, progress indicator, inventory alerts, concise dashboard metrics.

## Marketplace administrator
**Goal:** review vendors and products consistently and monitor marketplace health.
**Potential friction:** scattered moderation tasks, unclear application status, high-volume tables.
**Design opportunities:** status filters, clear evidence sections, confirmation dialogs, useful summary cards.

## Questions to validate
- What information helps customers trust an unfamiliar seller?
- Which checkout fields feel unnecessary or confusing?
- How should delivery estimates and returns policies be presented?
- Which vendor onboarding steps cause uncertainty?
- Which moderation actions need confirmation?
